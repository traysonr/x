PROJECT LOG / STARTED 2026.04

Solar-powered environmental logger

A low-power sensor node for recording temperature, humidity, and light in a remote garden bed. This project explores energy budgeting, robust outdoor packaging, and reliable data collection.

  • Embedded
  • Sensors
  • Low power

The logger is designed to run unattended through changing weather and intermittent sunlight. This page collects the design notes and test results as the hardware evolves.

First prototype and power budget

The first bench prototype uses a small microcontroller board, a digital temperature and humidity sensor, and a light sensor. The immediate goal is to measure sleep current before choosing a battery and panel.

03 JAN 2026 16:10

Outdoor enclosure and first field readings

I printed a vented enclosure with a downward-facing sensor opening and a separate sealed electronics compartment. The sensor sits behind a replaceable membrane to reduce splash exposure.

The first outdoor test ran for three days. The logger stayed online, though direct afternoon sun warmed the sensor by about 2 ?C. I?ll add a small radiation shield before treating the temperature data as representative.
